# MMM-STAGE2-BRIEF — Earned Numbers Above the Floor

**What Stage 1 (W3·G) got right and where it stops.** The floor is enforced (80 usable weeks, ≥2 varying channels), collinear channels report as *inseparable*, ROI is withheld below the floor, and the fit stores itself. But above the floor, three numbers are asserted rather than earned: the optimiser moves a flat 15% from worst to best **average** cost-per-outcome — ignoring the very saturation curves the model fit; its gain band is `mid×0.5 / mid×1.5` — a shape, not an estimate; and `holdoutMape` is computed and then *ignored*. Stage 2 is the discipline that makes the unlocked numbers trustworthy.

## The four Stage-2 laws

**1. The skill gate.** Passing the data floor proves you have history, not that the model learned anything. The optimiser now speaks only when the fit **beats a naive baseline** (train-mean predicted onto the holdout, same MAPE formula) — `holdoutMape < naiveMape`, recorded as `diagnostics.skill`. Above the data floor but below the skill floor, the optimiser is `null` and `diagnostics.optimiserNote` says exactly why. A model that cannot out-predict the mean of its own history has not earned a budget recommendation.

**2. Intervals are earned by resampling, never asserted.** A moving-block bootstrap (default B=120, block 8 weeks — blocks because weeks are autocorrelated) refits the same non-negative ridge on resampled panels. Per channel this yields `signalShare` — the fraction of resamples in which the coefficient survives the non-negativity projection — and a **cost-per-outcome range** (p10–p90 of resampled ROI). The PRNG is seeded (default 42, caller-settable) and the seed is stored with the run: an auditor can reproduce every interval.

**3. Per-channel abstention above the floor.** A channel whose sign is unstable (`signalShare < 0.7`) or that is collinearity-blocked gets `roi: null` — *withheld with its reason* — even when the run as a whole is above both floors. The Stage-1 point `costPerOutcome` keeps its exact legacy semantics (floor + non-collinear + positive contribution) so history and tests stay comparable; the **range** is the number that now carries authority, and the optimiser trusts only channels that earned one.

**4. The optimiser walks its own curves.** Reallocation is computed on the fitted response `coef · hill(adstock-steady · s)` per channel: equal-marginal coordinate transfers of the **same weekly budget**, each channel bounded to ±25% of its current weekly spend — the model is only trusted near where it was fit, and a recommendation to triple a channel is model overreach wearing a suit. The projected lift is evaluated per bootstrap coefficient draw, so `projectedGain {lo,mid,hi}` is a resampled percentile band (per week, plus a 12-week horizon). Total spend is conserved by construction. "No budget is changed" stays in the assumptions, verbatim — a suggestion for a human to weigh.

## Compatibility contract
Everything is additive: `contributions[]` gains `roi {lo,hi}|null` and `signalShare`; `diagnostics` gains `naiveMape`, `skill`, `bootstrap {B, blockLen, seed}`, `optimiserNote`; the optimiser keeps `projectedGain.{lo,mid,hi}` ordered and the legacy assumption line, or is `null` — the two shapes W3·G tests accept. Refusal paths below the data floor are untouched and skip the bootstrap entirely. **Zero schema changes** (Stage-2 payloads ride the existing jsonb columns; the optimiser is now also persisted inside `diagnostics`). Zero new dependencies — PRNG (mulberry32), Hill derivative, bootstrap, and the transfer loop are ~120 lines of plain JS.

## Proof obligations (tests, dedicated outcomeKeys so nothing leans on the legacy fixture)
Synthetic ground truth, 104 weeks, three channels — ALPHA (strong), BETA (weak), NOISE (β=0) — generated through the model's own transforms with a seeded RNG: readiness passes · skill gate passes · point-ROI ordering matches the planted truth · ALPHA and BETA carry ranges while **NOISE abstains** with low signalShare · optimiser conserves the total to the cent, caps every shift at ±25%, grows ALPHA, and lists only eligible channels · `lo ≤ mid ≤ hi` with `mid > 0` · same seed ⇒ same mid (determinism) · a second key with pure-noise outcome above the data floor ⇒ **skill false, optimiser null, note names the naive baseline** · both legacy above-floor tests stay green untouched.
